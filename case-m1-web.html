Marketing site copy

When M1 wanted to retool their brand and appeal to high-net-worth clients, I rewrote the marketing site top to bottom.

Client
M1
Scope
11 pages, two shown here
Constraints
FINRA 2210, new positioning

01 · The problem

New positioning, old branding

M1 is a finance app designed for long-term investors. Previously, the tagline was “Yours to build,” the design was brightly colored, and the voice skewed more quippy. To help M1 pivot toward serious, long-term investors, we developed a new tagline (“Sophisticated wealth building, simplified”), new look, and refreshed messaging.

02 · The solution

A full refresh

An elevated look with messaging to match. Copy that’s elegant yet still easy to read, benefits defined by their outcomes, and a quick overview of the platform designed to give the reader an “aha” moment.

Homepage hero, the new tagline in place. Click to open it full size.

Why decks instead of screenshots A screenshot of a landing page is hard to read, especially when it has interactive elements built in. The copy deck is the scaffolding that helps the designer build this page.

03 · Work sample

The homepage, annotated

Homepage

m1.com

H1

Sophisticated wealth building, simplified

Decision Two words carry seemingly opposing promises. That’s the new tagline, and it identifies the audience immediately. The reader wants to feel like a serious investor without being bogged down by busywork.

Subhead

At M1, we think a balanced portfolio shouldn’t be taxing. Invest for the years ahead without stressing day-to-day.

Note I tend to avoid jokes and puns in copy, but this one sets up the brand perfectly. Witty enough to engage a cold prospect, while still converting the ultimate benefit.

A page where the pictures move

Copy and motion Early steps teach vocabulary. Following slides build on the mechanism.

Section

Skip the real-time updates

Who needs second-by-second trading data when you’re in it for the long haul? M1 executes trades during scheduled trade windows. This takes the emotion out of timing your trades for a more disciplined approach.

Decision Sells a limitation as a benefit. Since trades only happen within windows, I position it as an advantage for people with a long-term mindset, in direct opposition to FinTechs that gamify trading.
